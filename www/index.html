Dual Heritage • Single Masterpiece

The Fusioni Alchemy

Italian cuisine honors simplicity, letting top-tier ingredients sing through slow reduction and hand craftsmanship. Indian royal kitchens master the geometry of spice, layer by delicate layer. At Fusioni, these traditions do not collide—they converse.

01. Wood-Fired Stone

Forged in Tuscan Clay & Olive Wood

Our 850°F Vesuvian volcanic stone oven produces smoky leopard spots on sourdough naan pizza bases, blistered heirloom tomatoes, and slow-braised osso buco spiced with green cardamom and saffron soot.

  • Double Zero Flour fermentations paired with Atta grains
  • Liguria extra virgin olive oil infused with roasted cumin
  • 24-Hour Parmigiano-Reggiano broths with star anise
02. Clay Tandoor

The Geometry of Dum & Tandoor

Traditional copper handis sealed with sourdough pastry create slow dum pressure. Italian carnaroli rice absorbs the essence of star anise, black cardamom, and aged Barolo wine for an unprecedented risotto-biryani hybrid.

  • Peshawari garam masala tempered in cultured ghee
  • Smoked Kashmiri chilies paired with San Marzano tomatoes
  • Fresh buffalo mozzarella smoked over cinnamon bark
