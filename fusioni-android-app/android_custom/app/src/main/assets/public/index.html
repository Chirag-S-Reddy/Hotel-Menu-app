Our Heritage

An Unstudied Dialogue Between Rome & Rajasthan

Founded by Chef Matteo Rossi and Chef Vikramaditya Singh, Fusioni represents a deliberate convergence of two rich culinary traditions. We apply classical Italian techniques to the complex spice profiles of India's historic trade routes.
